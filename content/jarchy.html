Jarchy

The Herald's Iradi-language weekly, every Thursday since 2017

Jarchy ("The Herald") is our weekly in Iradi for younger readers. It carries the week's news in translation, music and film, poetry and a page of readers' letters, Okurmandardın xattarı. Since its first issue in September 2017 it has grown with the Iradi language revival, the tilge kaytuu, a phrase our own essay series gave the language in 2016. It is written in the official Latin alphabet, so Jarçı inside the paper and Jarchy on the masthead, which has not changed since the first issue.

Jarchy also travels further than the Herald. Copies go by bus to the district towns and, near the line, by mounted courier to herding settlements, where many readers have no English.

Read Jarchy online: the whole of each Thursday's issue is now on this site at talasherald.com/jarchy, with a short note in English under every piece for readers who are still learning.

In every issue

  • Haftanın xabarları: the week's news in Iradi
  • Muzıka and Kino: Mor-Wave, Mor-Folk, new releases and films
  • Ir betı: the poetry page, open to readers
  • Okurmandardın xattarı: your letters, with replies
  • Haftanın sözü: a word of the week from the Talas State University dictionary

The editor is Aidai Kurmanalieva, who founded the paper in 2017.

The print edition costs 3 som at kiosks, and Herald subscribers receive it free with their Thursday paper. Write to Jarchy, in Iradi please, at jarchy@talasherald.com. Letters to the Herald in English go to the letters page.
