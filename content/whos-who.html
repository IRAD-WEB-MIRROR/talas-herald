Who's Who in Irad 2026–27

Our yearly biographical dictionary of notable Iradis. Thirty-first edition, revised to 1 October 2026.

Every year since 1995, with the single exception of 2007, the Herald has published Who's Who in Irad: short, plain entries on the men and women whose work or office makes them of public interest, in government, the Mejlis and the courts, business and the unions, the churches and the mosques, the clans, the arts and sport. Inclusion is decided by the editors alone; it cannot be bought and it implies neither praise nor blame.

This edition has 163 biographies with 51 portraits, and a Historical Section of 30 Iradis whose deaths have been recorded, from the founders of 1992 to the painters, writers and film-makers of the last century.
