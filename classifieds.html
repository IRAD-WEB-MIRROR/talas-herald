Deaths

ABDRAI KYZY, Ulbolsun, of Iyık Janyl, on 9 November, aged 94. Dear wife of the late Ishak, mother of 6, grandmother of many. Burial at the Iyık Janyl cemetery on Wednesday at 12:00. The family receive visitors at home.
