Müchöl: The cow does not hurry, and does not forget

By Anara Sooronbay in Culture on Saturday 21 November 2026

This Saturday belongs to the Cow, uy, and I will say at once that she has had poor press. Older people still call the Cow year an unlucky one, and my grandmother in Kara-Buura, like some highland grandmothers, would not even use the plain word. She said arkar jyly, the Year of the Argali, as if the cow needed a wilder, more respectable name before she could be spoken of at table. I never understood it. The cow has never asked to be anybody's omen. She asks for hay and a dry place to stand.

What the old sayings give her is patience, hard work and a stubborn streak. Those who take after the Cow are said to be slow to start and slower to stop. When a child planted both feet at the gate and would not be moved for sweets or threats, my grandmother would sigh and say, "That one was born with the yoke already on." She meant it as praise, though you had to listen carefully. A Cow-born child will carry the water bucket all the way home, and will tell you at length what is wrong with how you filled it.

The same sayings say the Cow is slow to forgive, and here I speak as a woman who knows one. She has not spoken to her sister-in-law since a borrowed cooking pot came back with a dent in it, and that was before the last Horse year. But the cow's memory is not only for grievances. She also remembers who opened the gate for her in a cold wind. If you want a Cow-born friend for life, do her one plain kindness and never mention it.

It is a good week to think of her. The stoves are lit and the mornings are dark and hard, and nobody wants to leave the quilt. The cow does not discuss it. She gets up, does what she did yesterday, and by noon the work is done. We are in jylky jyly, the Horse year, which everyone says is made for the road, and I am glad of it. But somebody has to pull the cart behind the horse, and the Cow will arrive with the load intact.

To my readers born in 2009 and 2021, and to every grandparent trying to bring one of them to supper: it is not obstinacy, it is character, and character takes years to bend. To those born in 1937, 1949, 1961, 1973, 1985 and 1997, you know already. Now read your twelve lines, and mend something this weekend. The Cow would.

The week for each year

Mouse (chychkan jyly; born 1960, 1972, 1984, 1996, 2008, 2020): You will spot the quick way round, as you always do. Before you take it, ask whether it leaves someone else to carry the heavy end.

Cow (uy jyly; born 1961, 1973, 1985, 1997, 2009, 2021): Finish the task you have been grumbling about, and then let one old grudge go home without its supper. You will not miss it as much as you think.

Tiger (jolbors jyly; born 1962, 1974, 1986, 1998, 2010, 2022): Lead from the front if you must, but lower your voice at the door. A household will follow you further if it is not frightened of you.

Hare (koyon jyly; born 1963, 1975, 1987, 1999, 2011, 2023): Make the house warm and see that the draught under the door is stopped. Then sit down, because the rest can wait until morning.

Fish (balyk jyly; born 1964, 1976, 1988, 2000, 2012, 2024): Keep the quiet counsel you are known for, and listen more than you speak at the table. Somebody near you is waiting to be asked how they are.

Snake (jylan jyly; born 1965, 1977, 1989, 2001, 2013, 2025): Take your time over a decision, for you are good at it. But do not let the waiting grow so long that a neighbour thinks you have forgotten them.

Horse (jylky jyly; born 1966, 1978, 1990, 2002, 2014, 2026): It is your year, and you will want to be off. Slow down once this week for someone who walks, and ask them what the road looks like from there.

Sheep (koy jyly; born 1955, 1967, 1979, 1991, 2003, 2015): Give what you can spare, as you always do, and then let somebody give something back to you. It is rude to refuse a kindness twice.

Monkey (mechin jyly; born 1956, 1968, 1980, 1992, 2004, 2016): Mend the gate hinge or the loose shelf you keep looking at. Your hands will be quicker than your promises.

Hen (took jyly; born 1957, 1969, 1981, 1993, 2005, 2017): Be up before the house and put the kettle on, and do not announce that you did it. The tea will taste the same, and the quiet will be a gift.

Dog (it jyly; born 1958, 1970, 1982, 1994, 2006, 2018): Keep watch over your own door, and keep it open when a neighbour knocks. A loyal heart is worth little if it only barks.

Pig (donguz jyly; born 1959, 1971, 1983, 1995, 2007, 2019): Share the pot, and the cooking, and the washing-up. Easy-going people are sometimes the ones who forget to ask for help.

Anara Sooronbay taught Iradi in Kara-Buura for thirty-one years. Müchöl appears every Saturday.
